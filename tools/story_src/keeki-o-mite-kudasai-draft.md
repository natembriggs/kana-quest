# ケーキを見てください / Please Watch the Cake — draft

Level L1, grammar G1. Original story, Claude Opus 5.5.

## Premise

A brand-new household robot does exactly what it is told, word for word.
Mum says 「ケーキを見てください」 meaning "keep an eye on the cake"; the robot
hears "look at the cake", and looks at it — steadily, carefully, while
four-year-old Kota eats it. Kota then tells the robot 「内緒ね」 ("it's a
secret"). The robot keeps the secret perfectly: when Mum asks, it says
nothing at all. It just looks at Kota, whose face is white with cream.

The joke works in English too, because "watch" has the same two meanings.

## Scene map

1. **Kitchen, afternoon.** The robot has just arrived. Mum, going out to the
   shops, gives it its first job: 「ケーキを見てください」. Goal: keep the cake
   safe. Consequence: Mum leaves; the robot is alone with the cake.
2. **Same kitchen, minutes later.** The robot stares at the cake. Kota
   (about four) eats a strawberry off it; the robot watches. Kota eats the
   whole cake; the robot watches very carefully. Change: the cake is gone and
   the robot has done "its job" perfectly. Consequence: Kota needs a cover.
3. **Same, straight after.** Kota tells the robot it's a secret; the robot
   agrees, word for word. Consequence: the robot now has a second literal
   instruction, which will collide with the first.
4. **Mum comes home.** She asks where the cake is. The robot keeps the secret
   — it says nothing — but it looks at Kota (looking is what it does), and
   Kota's creamy face gives him away. Twist: obeying both instructions to the
   letter is exactly what exposes him.

Continuity: the robot, the cake and Mum are introduced in scene 1; Kota in
the first sentence (his house). The robot's habit of doing literally what it
is told is set up in scene 1 and shown three times in scene 2 before it
pays off twice in scenes 3–4 (it keeps the secret; it looks).

## Japanese

こうたの家に新しいロボットが来ました。
お母さんは「ロボット、ケーキを見てください」と言いました。
それから、お母さんは買い物に行きました。

ロボットはケーキをじっと見ました。
こうたはいちごを一つ食べました。
ロボットはケーキを見ました。
こうたはケーキを全部食べました。
ロボットはケーキをとてもよく見ました。

こうたは「ロボット、内緒ね」と言いました。
ロボットは「はい、内緒です」と言いました。

お母さんが帰りました。
お母さんは「ケーキは？」と聞きました。
ロボットは何も言いませんでした。
ロボットはこうたをじっと見ました。
こうたの顔はクリームで真っ白でした。

## English

A new robot came to Kota's house.
Mum said, "Robot, please watch the cake."
Then Mum went out to the shops.

The robot stared at the cake.
Kota ate one strawberry.
The robot watched the cake.
Kota ate the whole cake.
The robot watched the cake very carefully.

Kota said, "Robot, it's a secret, OK?"
The robot said, "Yes. It is a secret."

Mum came home.
"Where's the cake?" Mum asked.
The robot said nothing at all.
The robot stared at Kota.
Kota's face was white with cream.

## Read-through notes

- 15 sentences, the L1 maximum. Every sentence one clause, です/ます only,
  plus direct quotation. 見てください inside Mum's quote is the one te-form;
  it is the whole premise, and a fixed request formula every beginner meets
  in week one.
- Rule of three in scene 2: stare / strawberry, watch / whole cake,
  watch very carefully. The robot's line grows more diligent as the cake
  shrinks.
- じっと見ました opens scene 2 and closes the story: the robot stared at the
  cake, and at the end it stares at Kota.
- Katakana: ロボット, ケーキ, クリーム — the words Japanese actually uses, and
  three an English speaker reads at once.
