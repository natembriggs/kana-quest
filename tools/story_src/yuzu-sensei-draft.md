# ゆず先生 / Teacher Yuzu — draft

Level L2, grammar ≤ G2. Original story, written by Claude Opus 5.5.
Warm sports comedy: a proud, secretly frightened grandfather is taught to
swim by his bossy eight-year-old granddaughter.

## Scene map

1. **Home, one Saturday.** Yuzu (8) swims well; Grandpa (70) cannot swim.
   Goal: Yuzu announces she will teach him. Obstacle: his pride — he hides
   behind the newspaper and says he simply doesn't *want* to swim.
   Change: the newspaper is upside down, so the reader (and Yuzu) can see
   he is rattled. Consequence: they go (the next scene opens at the pool).
2. **Memory, last spring.** Grandpa taught Yuzu to ride a bike: he held the
   back, said "I've got you", and she rode on and on, looked back, and he
   was far behind, waving. This is the rule the ending depends on,
   planted in full before the pool.
3. **Town pool, poolside.** Goal: get Grandpa into the water. Obstacle: he
   stands at the edge for ages and complains the water is cold. Change:
   Yuzu points out he hasn't got in yet. Consequence: he lowers himself in.
4. **In the water, the lesson.** Goal: face in the water, count to five.
   Obstacle: he dunks and pops straight up claiming "Five!"; Yuzu: "Still
   one." Change: on the third try he really does count to five (so he can
   now keep his face in). Consequence: next step, the flutter kick. Yuzu
   puts her hands under his tummy; he shuts his eyes tight and kicks. She
   says "I've got you" — Grandpa's own words — and quietly lets go.
5. **Payoff.** He swims five metres alone, stands, looks back: Yuzu is far
   behind, waving (mirroring scene 2 line for line). "That's cheating!"
   "You did the same with my bike." He roars with laughter and asks his
   teacher for one more go.

The ending is earned by two set-ups: the bike trick (scene 2), which gives
Yuzu her method and gives Grandpa no grounds to complain, and the
face-in-water counting (scene 4), which is what lets him keep going
without her.

## Japanese (prose)

ゆずは八歳で、泳ぎが上手です。ゆずのおじいちゃんは七十歳ですが、泳ぎができません。土曜日、ゆずは「私がおじいちゃんに泳ぎを教えます」と言いました。おじいちゃんは新聞を開いて、「泳ぎたくないだけです」と言いました。でも、新聞はさかさまでした。

去年の春、おじいちゃんはゆずに自転車を教えました。おじいちゃんは自転車の後ろを持って、「持っているよ」と言いました。ゆずは走って、走って、後ろを見ました。おじいちゃんはずっと後ろで手を振っていました。

町のプールで、おじいちゃんは長い間プールサイドに立っていました。「今日は水が冷たいですね」「おじいちゃん、まだ入っていませんよ」おじいちゃんはゆっくり水に入りました。

「まず、顔を水につけて、五まで数えましょう」おじいちゃんは顔をつけて、すぐに上げて、「五！」と言いました。「まだ一ですよ」三回目で、おじいちゃんはやっと五まで数えました。次に、ゆずはおじいちゃんのおなかの下に手を入れました。おじいちゃんは目をぎゅっとつぶって、バタ足をしました。ゆずは「持っているよ」と言って、そっと手を離しました。

おじいちゃんは五メートル泳いで、立って、後ろを見ました。ゆずはずっと後ろで手を振っていました。おじいちゃんは「ずるい！」と言いました。「おじいちゃんも、自転車の時、そうしましたよ」おじいちゃんは大きな声で笑って、「先生、もう一回お願いします」と言いました。

## English

Yuzu is eight, and she is a good swimmer. Her grandpa is seventy, but he
cannot swim. One Saturday, Yuzu said, “I’m going to teach you to swim,
Grandpa.” Grandpa opened his newspaper and said, “I just don’t want to swim,
that’s all.” But the newspaper was upside down.

Last spring, Grandpa had taught Yuzu to ride a bike. He held on to the back
of the bike and said, “I’ve got you.” Yuzu pedalled and pedalled, and then
she looked back. Grandpa was far behind her, waving.

At the town pool, Grandpa stood at the poolside for a long time. “The
water’s cold today, isn’t it?” “Grandpa, you haven’t even got in yet.”
Slowly, Grandpa got into the water.

“First, put your face in the water and count to five.” Grandpa put his face
in, lifted it straight out again, and said, “Five!” “That was only one.” On
the third try, Grandpa finally counted all the way to five. Next, Yuzu put
her hands under Grandpa’s tummy. Grandpa squeezed his eyes shut and did a
flutter kick. Yuzu said, “I’ve got you,” and quietly let go.

Grandpa swam five metres, stood up and looked back. Yuzu was far behind him,
waving. “That’s cheating!” said Grandpa. “You did that too, Grandpa —
with my bike.” Grandpa laughed out loud and said, “One more time, please,
Teacher.”

## Continuity notes

- 25 sentences. Every beat follows by cause or clear time/place change:
  announcement → refusal (pride) → tell (upside-down paper) → flashback
  (marked 去年の春) → pool (marked 町のプールで) → lesson → payoff.
- 持っているよ appears exactly twice, once from each teacher; ずっと後ろで
  手を振っていました appears exactly twice, once for each pupil.
- Why he can keep his face down while kicking: the counting drill.
- Why she can let go without him sinking at once: he is kicking, face in,
  in shallow water (he stands up at the end).
